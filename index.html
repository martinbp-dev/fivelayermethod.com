The Five-Layer Method™ is a synthesis of what actually works in neuroscience and psychology, stripped back and sequenced in the order the body can receive it.

The shortest honest route to nervous system regulation and real change.

The method

Five layers. Always in this order.

Every session moves through the same sequence. The order is the method.

  1. 1

    Arrive

    Regulate

    Three slow sighs. Double inhale, long exhale. The body settles before anything is asked of it.

  2. 2

    Notice

    The Good Sweep

    A guided sweep of what is already good. The body's quiet work, the love you have received, ordinary wonder.

  3. 3

    Lift

    The Charge

    An elevated emotion, matched to the day's belief, felt in the heart until it is warm and real.

  4. 4

    Rewrite

    The Update

    The day's belief, updated at the root. Not an affirmation laid on top. A change underneath.

  5. 5

    Direct

    The Becoming

    The last minutes belong to the person the new belief describes. You leave the practice already being them.
